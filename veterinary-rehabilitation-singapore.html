A clear plan for better movement and comfort

Veterinary rehabilitation supports pets living with mobility problems, pain, weakness or reduced function after injury, surgery or illness. At ARK, every plan begins with an assessment and is adjusted to the individual pet rather than applying the same programme to every patient.

Our existing services include therapeutic exercise, hydrotherapy, laser therapy, TENS and NMES, acupuncture, nutrition support and house visits. These modalities can be combined when appropriate, with practical guidance for owners who continue selected exercises or home-care steps between clinic sessions.

What rehabilitation at ARK can include

Treatment may include hands-on therapy, tailored exercises, pain-management modalities, underwater treadmill work and changes to the home environment. The chosen combination depends on the pet’s condition, tolerance and goals.

ARK also provides follow-up guidance and home rehabilitation recommendations where appropriate. Suitability and session frequency are decided after veterinary assessment; individual outcomes vary.

Questions

What pet owners often ask.

What is veterinary rehabilitation?

It is a personalised programme that uses assessment, therapeutic exercise and selected rehabilitation modalities to support mobility, comfort and function.

Does every pet receive the same therapies?

No. ARK builds the plan around the individual pet’s condition, tolerance, lifestyle and stage of healing.

Can owners continue rehabilitation at home?

Where appropriate, the team teaches selected exercises and home-care steps so owners can support the clinic plan between sessions.
