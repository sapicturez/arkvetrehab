Bringing rehabilitation care to your pet

Travel can be difficult for older, painful, anxious or mobility-limited pets. ARK’s house-visit service brings a rehabilitation veterinarian to the pet’s own environment, allowing assessment and treatment without the journey to the clinic.

A visit can include a full assessment, customised planning, manual techniques, tailored exercises and electrotherapy where indicated. The session is shaped around the pet’s comfort, home layout and practical mobility goals.

The home itself can inform the plan

Seeing the pet at home can reveal practical barriers such as slippery flooring, difficult transitions or areas where supportive surfaces and simple environmental changes may help. ARK can also demonstrate selected exercises or handling techniques in the place where owners will use them.

Some services and equipment remain clinic-based, including the underwater treadmill. The team will explain whether home visits, clinic appointments or a combination is most appropriate.

Questions

What pet owners often ask.

What can an ARK house visit include?

A visit may include assessment, treatment planning, manual therapy, tailored exercise and electrotherapy where indicated.

Who may benefit from a house visit?

It can suit pets that travel poorly, owners with mobility limitations and cases where assessing the home environment is useful.

Can hydrotherapy be performed at home?

The underwater treadmill is clinic-based. ARK can advise whether to combine house visits with clinic appointments.
